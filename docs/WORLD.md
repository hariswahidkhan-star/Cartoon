# THE WORLD OF AERON — *The Lantern Sea*

## The shape of the world

There is no ground. There never was. Aeron is a **vertical world** — tiers of floating
isles suspended in an endless sky, stacked from the warm, low **Lantern Sea** at the
bottom to the cold, bright **Crown** at the top. People live on the isles. Everything
important in this story is a journey *upward*.

```
            ☀  THE CROWN        — where the Dawn is born (Act III)
          ·  ·  ·  ·            — the high, thin, cold isles
        isle   isle   isle      — the middle tiers (Act II: the climb)
      isle   isle   isle   isle — green, lived-in, bustling
   ────────── REN'S ISLE ──────── — the lowest, poorest tier (Act I)
  ~~~~~~~ THE LANTERN SEA ~~~~~~~ — the glowing ocean of risen souls
```

## The rule of souls (the heart of the world)

1. When someone dies, their soul becomes a **lantern** — a warm floating light holding
   their memories. It drifts slowly upward.
2. Most people can only *see* the lanterns. A rare few — like **Ren** — can *hear* the
   memories inside them.
3. Every generation, all the lanterns answer a pull and gather into the **Long Tide**, a
   vast glowing river flowing up to the Crown.
4. At the Crown, the Tide is reborn as the **Dawn** — the living warmth that keeps the sea
   lit so new lanterns can keep rising. It is the engine of the whole cycle of life.

## The crisis (why now)

The last Dawn is dying of old age. The sea is **dimming**. Lanterns have started to **fall
instead of rise** — and a fallen lantern is a soul *lost forever*, not reborn. This Long
Tide is the final one with enough light left to make a new Dawn. If it fails, the world
goes dark and every soul, living and dead, ends.

Suzu is that Tide, given a body. She is the last chance. She just doesn't know it yet.

## Texture & culture (for backgrounds and incidental life)

- **Lamp-keepers** like Ren tend the great lighthouses that help lanterns find the Tide.
  A dying profession in a dimming world.
- **Lantern festivals** — communities release paper lanterns to "walk the dead home." Our
  Act II lantern-dance grows out of this. (The *Tangled* beat, reinvented.)
- **The fallen-light shrines** — quiet, grief-struck places where people mourn lanterns that
  dropped into the dark. The stakes, made visible.
- **Isle ecologies** — each tier has its own weather, plants, and light. The climb should
  feel like crossing whole countries: a rain-isle, a wind-isle, a half-abandoned isle, a
  blossoming isle. Every stop = a new wonder (and a new background painting to show off).

## Rules we must never break (internal consistency = feels real)

- The dead rise; only the saved and the lost fall. Direction *means* something. Never waste it.
- Suzu's glow tracks her humanity: brighter = more cosmic/light, softer = more human/alive.
- A lantern can only be *heard*, never kept or brought back. Grief in this world is about
  listening, not holding. (This is the lesson Ren must learn.)
