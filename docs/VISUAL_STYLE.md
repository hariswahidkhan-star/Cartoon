# VISUAL STYLE & ART DIRECTION — *The Lantern Sea*

The look must say "premium" in the first frame. Reference the masters, then find our own.

## North-star references

- **CoMix Wave / Makoto Shinkai** (*Your Name*, *Weathering With You*) — photo-real skies,
  god-tier lighting, lens flares, that aching golden-hour glow. **This is our base.**
- **Studio Ghibli** (*Spirited Away*, *Castle in the Sky*) — hand-painted backgrounds,
  floating worlds, warmth, wonder. (Our floating isles are a direct spiritual descendant of
  *Castle in the Sky*.)
- **Ufotable** (*Demon Slayer*) — digital compositing fireworks for the big spectacle beats
  (the Long Tide, the Dawn).

## The signature image

**Warm light against vast dark.** Every frame is a small human warmth (a lamp, a face, a
lantern) held inside an enormous, beautiful, indifferent space (the sea, the sky, the dark).
That contrast *is* the theme, painted. If a shot doesn't have it, reconsider the shot.

## Color script (per act)

| Act | Palette | Emotion |
|---|---|---|
| I — The Girl Who Fell | cold blues, deep indigo, one warm amber lamp | loneliness, a single hope |
| II — The Climb | the palette *blooms* — teal, rose-gold, green, warm sun | life, love, discovery |
| III — The Crown | near-black sea, then a single overwhelming golden Dawn | grief → transcendence |

The whole film is a journey **from one small light in the dark → to the whole sky alight.**

## Key visual motifs (use relentlessly)

- **Lanterns** — our *Tangled* lantern moment, but it's the entire world. Never let them
  feel ordinary.
- **Glow as emotion** — Suzu glows brighter when she feels; dimmer as she becomes human.
  The audience learns to read her heart as light.
- **Up vs. down** — the dead rise, the saved fall, she's pulled up, he wants her to stay
  down. Compose vertically. Let the frame *ache* upward.
- **Water-that-is-sky** — reflections, drifting hair, floating debris. The sea and sky are
  one substance; blur the line constantly.

## Technical look (for the AI/digital pipeline)

- 2D cel-shaded characters over **painterly, high-detail backgrounds** (the Shinkai recipe).
- Heavy use of **bloom, volumetric light, god-rays, bokeh particles** (lantern dust).
- **2.39:1 anamorphic** aspect ratio — this is a *film*, not a TV show. Widescreen scope.
- 24 fps, animated "on twos/threes" for characters (anime cadence), smooth for effects.

## What to lock before production

1. **Character model sheets** (front/side/back + expressions) for Ren and Suzu — the single
   most important art asset. Consistency here = looks professional. Everything else drifts
   without it.
2. **3–5 "key frame" paintings** of the signature images (the fall, the lantern dance, the
   Dawn). These are your pitch, your mood, your target quality bar.
3. **The color script** above, as actual painted swatches per sequence.
