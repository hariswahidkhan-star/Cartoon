# THE LANTERN SEA — Screenplay

### ACT I · Sequence 1 — "The Keeper" (opening ~4 minutes)

*Format: feature screenplay. This is the opening that has to hook the world in 4 minutes —
establish the world, the hero, the ache, and the rule, without a single line of clumsy
exposition.*

---

FADE IN:

**BLACK.**

A sound first — a soft, low *hum*, like a hundred distant voices almost singing. Warm.
Lonely.

SUPER: *"They say the sky remembers everyone it has ever loved."*

**EXT. THE LANTERN SEA — NIGHT**

We open on an impossible, breathtaking image: an **ocean made of light** — a slow,
glowing sea of cloud stretching to every horizon. And rising from it, drifting upward into
a star-choked sky, **thousands of warm floating lanterns.** Each one a soul. Each one humming.

It is the most beautiful thing we have ever seen. It is also, we slowly notice, too dark at
the edges. Some lights, far off, are *falling.*

The camera descends — down, down, below the other isles — to the **lowest, smallest,
loneliest rock** in the whole sky. On it: one crooked **lighthouse.** One warm window.

**INT. LIGHTHOUSE — LAMP ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

**REN** (17), sleeves rolled, soot on one cheek, trims a wick with the care of a prayer. He
strikes a match. The great lamp *blooms* — a single warm beam sweeps out across the dark sea.

He does this like he has done it ten thousand times. Because he has.

He leans on the rail, chin on his arms, and *listens.* Out in the sea, the lanterns drift
past. And Ren — alone of almost everyone in the world — can **hear** them.

WHISPERS (overlapping, from the lanterns)
*...the bread is burning, love...  ...run, the tide's coming in...  ...I named her after
your mother...*

He smiles at them, small and sad, like you'd smile at old neighbors.

REN
(quietly, to a passing light)
Evening, Mr. Aldo. ...No, I haven't found her yet.

He watches that one drift up and away. Then he scans the sea — searching, always searching —
for one particular light among the thousands.

REN (CONT'D)
(barely a breath)
Still looking, Mum.

He doesn't find her. He never finds her. He lights the lamp anyway.

**EXT. LIGHTHOUSE — NIGHT**

A small lantern — *tiny*, a child's — drifts near Ren's isle. But it's low. Too low. It
**sinks** instead of rising, dimming toward the black beneath the sea.

Ren is already moving — out the door, down the rocks, reaching —

REN
No no no — come on — up, you go *up* —

His fingers close on cold air. The little light *winks out* below him. Gone. Not risen.
*Lost.*

Ren kneels at the edge of his world, staring into the dark where a soul used to be. We
understand everything now: the sea is *dying.* And he can't save any of them.

REN (CONT'D)
(to the dark, cracking)
...I'm sorry.

**EXT. THE SKY — NIGHT — MOMENTS LATER**

Then — far above — a bell-tone, impossibly pure. The whole sea *shivers* with light.

Every lantern in the world turns, as one, and begins to flow in a single direction —
**upward**, gathering into a luminous river climbing the sky toward the distant **Crown.**

Ren stands, breath caught, lamp-light on his face.

REN
(whisper)
The Long Tide...

It's the most light he's ever seen. It's also, he knows, the *last* of it.

And then — wrong. Impossibly wrong. One light breaks from the great rising river and
**falls.** Not sinking-dim like the lost ones — *streaking*, blazing, alive — straight down
out of the Tide, trailing gold, screaming toward the sea—

—toward **Ren's isle.**

He throws an arm up against the glare—

**SMASH TO:**

**EXT. SHORE OF THE SEA — NIGHT**

Steam. Stillness. The glow fading. And there, washed up on the glowing shore of the
Lantern Sea, faintly shining, is a **girl.**

She opens her eyes — eyes full of soft light — and looks at Ren as if she has been falling
toward him her whole life. She doesn't know her name. She doesn't know what she is.

She only knows, with her whole strange glowing heart, that she has to go **up.**

**SUZU**
(first words, wondering)
...you can hear them too?

Off Ren's face — a boy who has spent his life listening to the dead, finally looked at by
someone *alive* —

SMASH TO BLACK.

TITLE: **THE LANTERN SEA**

*(The hum resolves, for one held breath, into a single clear voice — a woman's — saying:
"...there you are.")*

---

### Director's notes on the opening

- **No narration dump.** The world's rule (souls rise as lanterns; some now fall and are
  lost) is shown through Ren's ritual and the dying child's lantern. The audience *feels*
  the stakes before they understand them.
- **The ache is established in 4 minutes:** Ren is kind, lonely, grieving, and powerless —
  so when Suzu arrives, she is literally the first living hope in his life. We're already
  terrified of losing her, and we just met her.
- **The last image before the title** plants the ending: the voice that says *"there you
  are"* is Ren's mother — and it pays off in the final scene when he hears her again, in the
  new Dawn. Set it up here, land it two hours later, and the theater weeps.
